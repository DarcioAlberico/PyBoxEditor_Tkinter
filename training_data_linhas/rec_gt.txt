images/pagina_0011_linha_00012.png	It is common for readers of chess books to prefer verbal explanations over lots ofvariations. Often
images/pagina_0011_linha_00013.png	analysis is disregarded with the contemptuous description of ''computer analysis''. Not always
images/pagina_0011_linha_00014.png	without Justification. It is easy to press the space bar and fill the white spaces on the page.
images/pagina_0011_linha_00015.png	This is not what has happened in this book. The variations are meant to be an illustration of
images/pagina_0011_linha_00016.png	possible play or to explain the tactical or technical nuances or the games. I find that it is easy to
images/pagina_0011_linha_00017.png	make a statement and get the approval of the reader. Usually, those who read a book are willing
images/pagina_0011_linha_00018.png	to believe that you know what you are talking about and will accept the evaluations as valid. But
images/pagina_0011_linha_00019.png	I am not looking for approval. I am wishing for you to improve your game.
images/pagina_0011_linha_00020.png	me variations and analysis in this book are thus illustrations and explanations and going
images/pagina_0011_linha_00021.png	through them will be a part of the educational process. The thematic explanation can be easy to
images/pagina_0011_linha_00022.png	follow conceptually but without seeing what it means in practice, I doubt it will have any positive
images/pagina_0011_linha_00023.png	influence on your game. There are a few times where the variations will be quite extensive and
images/pagina_0011_linha_00024.png	if you want, you can skip them. The more experienced you are, the less this should be the case.
images/pagina_0011_linha_00025.png	If you are a less experienced player and some variations seem overpowering, you should skip
images/pagina_0011_linha_00026.png	whatever you feel you need to skip. As with o ur academy it is never the intention that everyone
images/pagina_0011_linha_00027.png	should understand everything, but that everyone will learn something.
images/pagina_0011_linha_00028.png	If you can, I recommend that you go through the variations in your head. This is good for
images/pagina_0011_linha_00029.png	your training in visualisation in general and where chess should be played. If you need to play
images/pagina_0011_linha_00030.png	through the games on a board, I recommend that you still try to go through the variations in
images/pagina_0011_linha_00031.png	your head. Many strong players would never think of using a board when going through a book;
images/pagina_0011_linha_00032.png	others would not consider reading a book without it. Personally I am somewhere in-berween,
images/pagina_0011_linha_00033.png	depending on the book and my mood.

images/pagina_0012_linha_00018.png	3. Fortresses
images/pagina_0012_linha_00019.png	A topic unique to endgames and difficult to understand for a lot of the players I have worked

images/pagina_0012_linha_00021.png	basis, but rather on schematic thinking and a special sensibility for fortresses.
images/pagina_0012_linha_00022.png	4. Rook vs Bishop
images/pagina_0012_linha_00023.png	In this chapter we shall go deeper in to an endgame that most or the time ends with fortresses.

images/pagina_0012_linha_00026.png	to understand fully. I have done my best to do so, and explain what I have understood, but
images/pagina_0012_linha_00027.png	certainly not every aspect of this six-piece ending has been uncovered.
images/pagina_0012_linha_00028.png	5. Exchanges
images/pagina_0012_linha_00029.png	When and what to exchange is a difficult topic in chess and no less so in the endgame. In this
images/pagina_0012_linha_00030.png	chapter we shall debate this topic in depth, and why the strategic concept I was taught when I
images/pagina_0012_linha_00031.png	was younger is debateable. In going deep into this topic, I hope I can rescue you trom some of
images/pagina_0012_linha_00032.png	the disasters that befell my friends.
images/pagina_0012_linha_00033.png	6. Illustrative Games
images/pagina_0012_linha_00034.png	The final chapter includes a few recent games and two lesser-known games that feature big
images/pagina_0012_linha_00035.png	endgame battles. The concep t is to show the endgame in its natural habitat and go deep. I enjoy
images/pagina_0012_linha_00036.png	both small clear examples with one-dimensional explanations, and deep analysis of complicated
images/pagina_0012_linha_00037.png	games that tries to address all the elements we encounter on the battlefield.
