3.5.3: Black’s king is cut off along a file and Whiteis king is cut off along a rank

This is the most complicated section in the current chapter. White only has chances to win if Black’s king is cut off on the h-file, and then the result de- pends on the exact position of the pieces, particularly the location of Black’s king. The possible positions may be divided into three main classes. In the first class Black’s king is on h6, h7 or h8. In the second class the king stands on h4 or h5 and in the third class it stands on h3. Within a class the exact position of Black’s king is rela- actively unimportant, i.e. if White is winning in a position with Black’s king on h7 then he will also win if Black’s king is moved to h6 or h8.
